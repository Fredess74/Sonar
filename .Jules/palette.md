## 2024-05-18 - Timeline Item Semantic Buttons
**Learning:** In the `Sidebar` and `BottomSheet` components, timeline route nodes were originally interactive `div` elements, violating a11y standards. For sequential lists like these, applying `<button type="button">` with `w-full text-left` explicitly mimics the block-level structure of the `div` without disrupting existing layouts. Also, setting `aria-current="step"` is highly appropriate for sequential guided route paths.
**Action:** Always prefer `button type="button"` over `div` for interactive list items. Specifically in this application's design system, adding `focus-visible:ring-sonar-accent` effectively connects focus tracking directly to the app's established Cyberpunk thematic palette without needing additional custom CSS logic.
